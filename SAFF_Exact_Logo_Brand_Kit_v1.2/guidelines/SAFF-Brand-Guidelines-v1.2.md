# SAFF — Brand Guidelines v1.2

## Approved master artwork
The supplied presentation image is the single source of truth for the approved logo. This kit reproduces that exact direction: three queue participants, two graphite-grey figures and a lime-green leading figure; a vertical divider; the SAFF wordmark; and the tagline “Your turn. On your time.” The logo has not been redesigned.

## Logo files
- `logos/saff-primary.svg`: vector-traced master lockup from the approved image.
- `logos/saff-primary-transparent.png`: transparent raster master crop.
- `logos/saff-symbol.svg`: queue symbol only.
- `logos/saff-symbol-monochrome.svg`: one-color symbol.
- `logos/saff-symbol-transparent.png`: transparent symbol crop.
- `app-icons/saff-app-icon-512.png`: app icon crop from the approved presentation.

## Colors
- Midnight: `#0B0B0F`
- Lime: `#A6FF00`
- White: `#F5F5F5`
- Graphite: `#777B84`
- Muted text: `#A1A1AA`
- Surface: `#17171D`

## Typography
Use Plus Jakarta Sans for Latin text and IBM Plex Sans Arabic for Arabic. The lettering in the supplied wordmark is custom artwork and should not be retyped.

## Usage rules
- Use the supplied SVG as the preferred scalable asset.
- Preserve proportions, colors, spacing and lockup arrangement.
- Do not redraw the people mark, change the leading figure color, skew, stretch, add shadows, gradients, outlines or extra barber imagery.
- Use the standalone symbol for compact contexts; use the full lockup where there is room.
- On dark surfaces, use the primary lockup. On light surfaces, place the approved transparent asset on a clean light field only if contrast remains sufficient; do not recolor the artwork without approval.
- Keep clear space around the logo at least equal to the diameter of the largest head circle.

## Brand voice
Clear, calm, helpful, time-respecting. Core tagline: “Your turn. On your time.” Arabic line in the reference: “دورك، في الوقت المناسب لك.”

## Product design
Dark-first interface; lime is reserved for primary actions and the active/next-in-line state. Graphite is supporting queue information. Always pair status colors with labels/icons.

## Important technical note
The SVG files are contour-traced vector reproductions of the supplied raster reference. They are suitable for normal web use and scaling, but the custom wordmark is not a separately editable font/text object. For trademark filing or large-format production, have a vector designer inspect and refine the traced contours against the approved reference.
